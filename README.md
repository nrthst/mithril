# Mithril

Home of **[Slack of the Rings](slack-of-the-rings/)**: a Slack bot with ten slash commands that answer your team with a fitting line from Middle-earth.

To use it, click **Code → Download ZIP** above (or clone the repository) and follow the [project README](slack-of-the-rings/README.md). Setup takes about five minutes, and the bot runs on your own computer.

An unofficial, fan-made project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Tolkien Estate, Middle-earth Enterprises or Warner Bros.
